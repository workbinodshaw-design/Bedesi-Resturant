How do I order for home?

Open our online menu, add your dishes and submit your delivery details. Your request goes directly to the restaurant’s order panel. Our team will confirm it with you.

Where do you deliver?

We’re based in Kokrajhar. Add your address at checkout so our team can confirm delivery availability and charges for your location.

Can I order vegetarian dishes?

Yes. Use the Veg filter in our menu to explore vegetarian options. For allergies or specific ingredients, call the restaurant before ordering.

Is payment collected on this website?

No. The restaurant confirms your order, delivery charges and payment arrangements directly.
