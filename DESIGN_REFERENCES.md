# Historical design references

Current website setup, palette, entry points and operational limits are documented in README.md and WEBSITE_HANDOFF.md. Earlier QR-menu design references are retained below for context.

The user explicitly selected the lavender Mood / Traditional menu design in `codex-clipboard-10534c1f-32b7-44c8-8361-0bbf0194dbdb.png` as the final visual target.

Implemented visual structure:

- Compact search toolbar with a dark filter button and actual active-filter count.
- Purple food highlight carousel with working controls.
- Mood / Traditional menu tabs.
- Yellow, pink and blue mood cards on the home view, plus a six-tile expanded browsing view.
- Staggered recommended cards with a small View all card.
- Lavender dish-detail sheet, large isolated food visual and quantity selector.
- Floating charcoal cart with real selected-dish thumbnails and count, opening the separate order-preview page.

## Restaurant customization

The user subsequently requested restaurant categories in place of the reference's mood labels. The approved layout and sampled colours remain. The tiles now show Starters, Main course, Biryani, Pizza, Burgers and Desserts, with code-native food illustrations. Tabs are Categories and Full menu. Poppins is used for headings and product labels; Manrope is used for body text, controls and prices. Main course replaces the previous Curries display category throughout the demo catalogue.
